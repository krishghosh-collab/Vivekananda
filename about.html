Summary

Vivekanada pustakalaya is widely recognized as a well-established, privately owned bookstore and retail shop rather than a traditional public historical institution.

History

Establishment & Location:

Operating out of the Dhupguri Head Office (HO) area, the shop has spent years serving the educational needs of the local Jalpaiguri community


Evolution:

It started as a small, local resource provider and gradually built a strong foothold in the regional retail book industry by adapting to the growing requirements of students and competitive exam aspirants.


Naming Context:

It is important to note that while there are standard library clubs in the broader region—such as the historical Jalpaiguri Ramakrishna Ashram Free Reading Room and Library or the Vivekananda Library in Pramod Nagar—this specific venue in Dhupguri function primarily as a vital commercial book depot

Achievement

Top Regional Rating:

The establishment holds a consistent customer satisfaction rating of 4.1 out of 5 stars on regional directories like Justdial, highlighting its status as a trusted local book hub

Academic Support:

It acts as a primary, one-stop destination for text, reference, and academic resources, directly supporting the literacy and schooling ecosystem in Dhupguri.

Customer-Centric Policies:

The business has successfully sustained a vast and growing consumer base by implementing accessible distribution models, including flexible book procurement options for students.
